Critical Calls

Emergency Detection and Escalation

Not every HVAC call is a routine tune-up. A family with no AC at 104 degrees is a different situation entirely. Outscales recognizes the signals of an emergency and treats it accordingly.

High-urgency calls are flagged, prioritized, and routed to you immediately so the most valuable jobs are never handled at the same pace as a maintenance inquiry.

Urgency recognition
The system identifies emergency language and responds with appropriate priority.
Immediate escalation

Premium job capture
